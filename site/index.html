The product

Make the first editorial pass dramatically less manual.

The goal isn't to remove the editor. It's to move the editor closer to the decisions that matter.

01

Understand

Transcribe long-form footage and turn raw media into structured, searchable editorial material.

02

Find the story

Group moments, identify potential story units and rank material for editorial review.

03

Shape the cut

Analyze filler and dead air, reframe for vertical formats and prepare captioned outputs.

04

Keep it editable

Generate professional timeline artifacts so the result can remain inside an editor's workflow.

05

Keep humans in control

AI proposes the first pass. Editors review, change and approve the final story.

06

Learn over time

The long-term direction is an editorial intelligence layer that can learn how each team tells stories.

Engineering, not hype

We test the system by trying to break it.

SelekyT is still early. The public story should reflect what has actually been built, what has been measured, and what remains unproven.

“Mechanical correctness is not the same as editorial correctness.”

Real media testing has exposed failures that ordinary happy-path tests would miss — including media/container issues and multilingual transcription quality.

The next proof is not another feature. It is real editors saving real time.

Current validation is focused on real NLE import, transcription reliability, blinded editorial evaluation and measured time saved.

BuiltLocal-first editorial pipeline, Rust/Python core, web shell and desktop packaging work.
